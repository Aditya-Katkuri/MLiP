Revised VLM hazard baseline: sidewalk-data-3 test results

The validation-selected evidence_checklist prompt was applied unchanged to all
380 Pittsburgh test images. All 380 requests completed successfully, using
gpt-6.1-sol with low reasoning and the existing 224x224 RGB PNG inputs.
The hazard threshold remained fixed at 0.30; no test threshold or prompt search
was performed. The original four subtype probability outputs and their original
validation-selected thresholds were retained.

The revised hazard confusion counts are TP=107, FN=67, TN=133, FP=73.
Primary subtype metrics use all 174 true hazards, including hazards missed by
the binary detector. They are unchanged. Whole-pipeline metrics with subtype
decisions gated by the new hazard detector are saved separately.

The top-38 cutoff score is 0.90. Ten images share that score, and two are
included using the predeclared sample_id-ascending tie rule. Other tie orders
would yield 57.89%-63.16% precision@38. The top 46 includes all ten tied images,
so precision@46 has no cutoff-tie ambiguity.

Results were independently recomputed from the exported predictions, checking
all 380 identities, target labels, fixed threshold decisions, subtype score and
decision preservation, ranking counts, and the frozen selection fingerprint.
